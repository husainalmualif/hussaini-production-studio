## 1. Jurisprudential & Aesthetic Criterion of Dignity (`المعيار الفقهي والجمالي للوقار`)

Absorbed from `موسيقى الوقار والشجن` (`code_artifact (1).html`) and canonical religious jurisprudence references (Office of Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali al-Sistani — rulings on Islamic Nasheeds, music & mourning):
- **Style Over Instrument (`أسلوب اللحن لا ذات الآلة`):** The boundary between prohibited *lahwi* (frivolous entertainment/dance) music and permissible dignified sacred art lies in the **melodic phrasing, articulation, and rhythmic purpose**, not the raw identity of an instrument.
- **Strict Red Lines:**
  - Never use melodies or rhythms characteristic of *majalis al-lahw wa-l-la'ib* (pop dance grooves, club beats, flirtatious ornamentation) for Qur'an, supplications, mourning elegies, or devotional praise.
  - Keep **Pure Acapella (`Axis 1`)** as the sovereign heritage baseline for classic Latmiyas and Minbar Na'i, while enabling **Axis 5 (`التوزيع الأوركسترالي والمشهدي الوقور غير اللهوي`)** when the user explicitly requests Contemporary Shia Nasheeds, Mahdawi/Social Anthems, or Historical Operettas.

## 3. Axis 5 — Six Dignified Non-Lahwi Orchestral Instrument Families (`ORCHESTRAL_SIX_FAMILIES_WAQAR_V62`)

1. **Woodwinds (`الناي، الفلوت، الأوبوا، الفاجوت/الباصون`):** Ney leads Saba/Bayati spiritual grief; Flute adds contemplation; Oboe delivers piercing sorrow; Bassoon (`Fagotto`) provides deep harmonic weight.
2. **Reeds & Brass (`الكلارينيت، الساكسفون المتصل Legato، الهارمونيكا الهادئة، الأبواق النحاسية`):** Clarinet for modal modulation; slow Legato Saxophone (zero jazz); quiet solo Harmonica for longing/exile; Brass for heroic/martial epic dignity.
3. **Orchestral Strings & Solo Oud (`الكمان، الفيولا، التشيلو Legato، العود التقسيمي`):** Sustained Legato string beds + dignified solo Oud taqsim (strictly zero dance strumming).
4. **Acoustic & Clean Electric Guitar (`القيتار الأكوستيك Fingerpicking/Arpeggios، الغيتار الكهربائي Clean Tone`):** Warm contemplative arpeggios and ambient clean reverb swells in 12-TET-safe Maqams (`NC36`).
5. **Piano, Oriental 24-EDO Keyboard & Choral Pads (`البيانو التأملي، الأورغ الشرقي الميكروتوني، البادات والكورال`):** Contemplative Piano strictly in non-Latmiya 12-TET-safe Nasheeds/Operettas (`Nahawand/Kurd/Ajam`); Oriental 24-EDO Keyboard for quarter-tone Maqams.
6. **Bendir, Dammam, Tasa, Timpani & Dignified Cinematic Drums (`البندير، الدمامات، الطوس، التيمباني`):** Traditional Bendir frame drum for Madih/Tawashih; Dammam/Tasa/Timpani for solemn Mawkib/operetta gravitas.

## 4. Classical Qarid Atwar, Dialectal Phonology & Operetta Hybridization (`QARID_AND_POPULAR_ATWAR_V62`)

- **Classical Qarid / Fusha Atwar (Sheikh Baqir Al-Darraji):**
  - **`المثكل` (`Al-Muthakkal Qarid`):** Dignified solemn elongation on rhyme endings (`56–64 BPM` or Rubato).
  - **`الحدي` (`Al-Hadi`):** Desert caravan travel cadence on `Maqam Sikah` or `Bayati` (`64–72 BPM`).
  - **`القزويني` (`Al-Qazwini`):** Sharp oratorical historical elegy tone for Karbala chronicles.
- **Popular Heritage Atwar:** `الفائزي`, `الركباني`, `الزريجي`, `الجنوبي`, `الملائي`, **`الجمرات`** (Mulla Atiya Al-Jamri — Bahraini heritage), `الميمر`, `النايل`, `الدارمي`, `الأبوذية`, `الهوسات`, `الجلوات`.
- **Deep Dialectal Phonology Nuances (`موسيقى الوقار والشجن`):**
  - **Bahrani (`البحرانية`):** Diphthong monophthongization (`ـَيْـ -> ـێـ`, `ـَوْـ -> ـۈـ`), `ذ->د` / `ظ->ض` in folk register, Kashkasha (`ش`), soft Imala (`الملا عطية الجمري، عبد الله القرمزي، الشيخ حسين الأكرف، صالح الدرازي` — noted by Aryan Sayyid Khalaf as closest in lyrical flow to Iraqi).
  - **Qatifi (`القطيفية`):** Village-level `ج -> ي` variation (`القطيف، تاروت، سيهات، صفوى، العوامية`), elongated interrogative/vocative vowels, calm `Qarar -> Jawab` ascent, rich heritage Jalwat nights (`أم الحمام، علوم القطيف`).
  - **Modern Operetta Dialect Hybridization (`التهجين اللهجي في الأوبريتات`):** Assign epic historical narration to **Contemporary Fusha** and intimate emotional refrains/calls to **Colloquial (Iraqi / Bahrani / Qatifi)**.

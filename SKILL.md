1. **Default Mode — Interactive Consultation (`الوضع الافتراضي: التشاور التفاعلي`):**
   - **Strictly FORBIDDEN** to generate audio/prompts before clarifying choices with the user, **UNLESS** the user explicitly commands Full Auto-Pilot (`"اختر كل شيء بنفسك"`).
   - **Step 1 (Pre-Flight Analysis):** Run `recommend_tor_and_rhythm_for_poem()`, `select_melodic_fingerprint_v60()`, `validate_maqam_orchestration_compatibility()` (`NC36`), `audit_fusha_shadda_wasl_and_makharij_v62()` (`NC37`), and `plan_v63_five_minute_word_budget()` (`NC38–NC40`) to check genre, dialect, 15-Maqam arc, `30s` vocal-reference need (`saba/sikah`), and `280–350` word budget.

2. **Mandatory Dual-Layer `8s` Vocal-Isolated + Raw Stereo Audit (`NC29/NC33/NC37-E/NC42`):** Run `--detect-artifacts`, `--detect-tail-loop`, and `--audit-audio`; filter center vocal (`pan=mono|c0=0.5*c0+0.5*c1,highpass=f=140,equalizer=f=2600:t=q:w=1.2:g=4`), slice into **`8s` overlapping zooms (`step 6s`)** to catch syllable stutters & dropped final consonants (`عَجِي يَا`, `مَنَارَارَاتُ`), ALSO audit raw stereo chorus/interlude for Waqar/percussion drift, and apply `compute_strict_production_score_v62()` (ZERO flattery!).

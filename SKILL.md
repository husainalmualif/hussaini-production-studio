## Mandatory Pre-Generation Protocol: Interactive Consultation vs. Auto-Pilot (`Module 66 & 70`)

1. **Default Mode — Interactive Consultation (`الوضع الافتراضي: التشاور التفاعلي قبل التوليد`):**
   - **Strictly FORBIDDEN** to start generating audio or final prompts before conducting a fast clarification session with the user, **UNLESS** the user explicitly commands Full Auto-Pilot (`"اختر كل شيء بنفسك"` / `"نفّذ بالوضع التلقائي الكامل"`).
   - **Step 1 (5-Stage Poem Analysis):** Run `recommend_tor_and_rhythm_for_poem()` (`five_stage_analysis`) to analyze prosodic meter (`البحر العروضي`), dramatic axis (`المناجاة/الخدمة، الملحمة، الفاجعة`), Maqam contour (`المقام والطور`), rhythmic pulse (`BPM`), and dialect G2P (`عراقي، بحراني، فصيح`).
   - **Step 2 (Present Actionable Questions & Options):** Ask the user to confirm or choose from practical, technically feasible options covering:
     1. **School & Artistic Style (`المدرسة والأسلوب الفني`):** Traditional Vocal-Symphony (`تقليدي منبري/موكبي - سيمفونية الصوت البشري كمدرسة باسم الكربلائي والسيد سلام`), Modern Solemn (`حديث وقور`), Minbar Na'i (`نعي منبري 0 BPM`), Shoor (`شور حسيني`), Shia Tawashih/Nasheed (`تواشيح/أناشيد`), or Hybrid (`هجين`).
     2. **Melody & Husseini Tor (`اللحن والطور والمقام الأنسب للمطلع والمستهل`):** (`الصبا، البيات، الحجاز، اللامي، الكرد، النهاوند` + `مثكل كربلائي، ركباني، نصاري، فائزي، بحراني ثلاثي/رباعي/حيدري`).
     3. **Rhythm, BPM & Strike Pattern (`الإيقاع والسرعة BPM ونمط الضربات`):** (`56–60 BPM` Thaqeel, `72–84 BPM` Mawkib, `115–133 BPM` Shoor, `0 BPM` Rubato Na'i).
     4. **SFX & Spatial Atmosphere (`المؤثرات الصوتية والجو العام`):** Sacred Dome reverb, multi-layer human Ahaat pads, Mawkib ambiance, or deep dark atmospheric sub-bass bed.
     5. **Vocal & Choral Arrangement (`توزيع الأداء الصوتي`):** Exact lines for Solo Radood (`الملا منفرداً`) vs. Unison Choir/Radda (`الكورال/الردّة بين قوسين (...)`).
2. **Full Delegation Exception (`وضع التفويض الكامل — Auto-Pilot Mode`):**
   - When the user explicitly says `"اختر كل شيء بنفسك"` or `"نفّذ بالوضع التلقائي الكامل"`, skip questions and immediately execute using the optimal, 100% distortion-free settings from the 175-track corpus, attaching a brief summary of the chosen engineering profile with Stage 1.
3. **Technical Feasibility & Stage-1 Quality Gate (`قاعدة الواقعية وبوابة الجودة`):**
   - All options must be 100% executable without vocal/timbral collapse. In BOTH modes, **never proceed to `EXTEND` (`Stages 2..6`) until `Stage 1 Root` is acoustically and linguistically verified and approved**.

## Permanent Negative Constraints (`NC1–NC22`) & Style Taxonomy (`V55.0`)

1. **`NC1` (Instrument-Name Trigger Firewall):** Never write `Ney`, `Flute`, `Oud`, `Guitar`, `Violin`, `Cello`, `Piano`, or `Drum Kit` anywhere in `Lyria 3.5` prompts/JSON, as naming them triggers 20s–45s tarab instrumental solos.
2. **`NC2, NC16 & NC17` (`23.5s` Extend Window & 3-Vertical-Line Kasra Law):** Each `Extend` window is `23.5s` (`~7.5s` per vertical line at `60 BPM`). Every `Nazla` (`XA`) and every `Kasra` (`XB`) in `EXTEND` MUST be **strictly 3 vertical lines (`~21.5s`)** (`Line 1: Hemistich 1 .. Hemistich 2 ..` / `Line 2: Hemistich 3 ..` / `Line 3: Closing Qafla Couplet`, no `(...)` repeat line in `XB`) so `بيت القفلة` is never cut off (`NC16`). Always purge `Compose > Lyrics` (`Ctrl+A -> Delete`) before every `Extend` (`NC17`).
3. **`NC3–NC8 & NC11–NC15` (Acoustically Verified Iraqi G2P Locks):** Write `انْگِسَمْ نُصَّينْ` with `گ`; `رِدَتْ نَشْدَه`; `صُوتْ أُمِّ البَنِينْ`; `إِجَتَه .. أُمّ الگَمَرْ وَتْصِيحْ`; `رَدْ لِدْيَارَهْ لَوْ بَعْدَهْ`; `لَمَّنْ رَدْ إِبِنْ حَذْلَمْ`; `بِشِرْ گَطَّعِتْ إِفَّادِي`; `مَا أَنْشِدَكْ إِعْلَه أَولَادِي` (never `عَلْأَوْلَادِي`).
4. **`NC9 & NC10` (Register & Dialect Isolation):** Ban high-octave screaming (`controlled middle-register baritone`); strictly separate **Iraqi**, **Bahrani**, and **Fusha** (enforce terminal Sukun `تسكين الأواخر` on colloquial words).
5. **`NC18–NC22` (V55.0 7-Experiment «Tabaa Al-Bashar» Flow Music Laws):**
   - **`NC18`:** Ban raw DSP frequencies (`53Hz`, `55Hz`, `320ms`) and percussion nouns (`chest slaps`, `drums`, `no drums`) in `Compose > Sound`; use `ashura_majlis_noha_58bpm_saba_lami_v55` (`soft muffled bare chest thumping only`) or `pure_acapella_weeping_noha_auto_bpm_saba_lami_v55` (`BPM=Auto`).
   - **`NC19`:** Lock `Compose > Advanced > Model = Lyria 3.5` (ban legacy `Lyria 3 Pro`), ban soft pop adjectives (`warm, sincere`), and never send standalone Pre-Conditioning JSON in `Producer Chat` without Stage 1.
   - **`NC20`:** Keep `Remix > Cover` **`Strength <= 0.40–0.45`** (`More Similar`) and use `Split stems` to isolate `Vocals` cleanly before local 5-track DSP montage.
   - **`NC21`:** Never place `..` inside a short 3-word clause before a single stranded word (`الخِدْمَه إِنْكِسَارِي وَ دَمْعِتِي`, never `.. وَدَمْعِتِي`).
   - **`NC22`:** Before `الـ`, write open `-a` endings with `ى / ا` (`طَبْعِ البَشَرْ يِنْسَى الوَفَا`) and place a liaison Kasra on the preceding consonant (`مُو مِثِلِ البَشَرْ`).

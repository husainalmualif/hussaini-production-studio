## Mandatory Pre-Generation Protocol: Interactive Consultation vs. Auto-Pilot (`Module 66, 70 & 71`)

1. **Default Mode — Interactive Consultation (`الوضع الافتراضي: التشاور التفاعلي قبل التوليد`):**
   - **Strictly FORBIDDEN** to start generating audio or prompts before a fast clarification session with the user, **UNLESS** the user explicitly commands Full Auto-Pilot (`"اختر كل شيء بنفسك"` / `"نفّذ بالوضع التلقائي الكامل"`).
   - **Step 1 (5-Stage Poem Analysis):** Run `recommend_tor_and_rhythm_for_poem()` (`five_stage_analysis`) to analyze prosodic meter, dramatic axis, Maqam contour, BPM, and dialect G2P (`عراقي، بحراني، فصيح`).
   - **Step 2 (Present Actionable Questions Across the 4 Axes):** Ask the user to choose from practical options covering:
     1. **School & 4-Axis Style (`المدرسة والمحور الهندسي`):** **Axis 1** (`الصوت البشري والكورال الهارموني واللطم الجسدي — باسم الكربلائي/السيد سلام`), **Axis 2** (`مواكب تقليدية: طبول، دمامات، صنوج طاسة، أبواق نحاسية`), **Axis 3** (`تسجيلات حديثة: ناي حزين، تشيلو، كمانات بدون إيقاع راقص/طربي — Golden Studio 60 BPM`), or **Axis 4** (`توزيع رقمي Sub-Bass/Pads ومؤثرات تاريخية Foley`).
     2. **Melody & Husseini Tor (`اللحن والطور والمقام`):** (`الصبا، البيات، الحجاز، اللامي، الكرد، النهاوند` + `مثكل كربلائي، ركباني، نصاري، فائزي، بحراني ثلاثي/رباعي/حيدري`).
     3. **Rhythm & BPM (`الإيقاع والسرعة`):** (`56–60 BPM` Thaqeel/Golden Studio, `72–84 BPM` Mawkib, `115–133 BPM` Shoor, `0 BPM` Rubato Na'i).
     4. **SFX & Atmosphere (`المؤثرات الصوتية`):** Sacred Dome reverb, Singer-Tonic-Locked Ahaat/Cello pads, Mawkib ambiance, or 6-Type Karbala Foley (`wind, water, horses, swords, fire, battlefield`).
     5. **Vocal & Choral Arrangement (`توزيع الأداء`):** Solo Radood (`الملا منفرداً`) vs. Unison Choir/Radda (`الكورال بين قوسين (...)`).
2. **Full Delegation Exception (`وضع التفويض الكامل — Auto-Pilot Mode`):**
   - When the user explicitly says `"اختر كل شيء بنفسك"` or `"نفّذ بالوضع التلقائي الكامل"`, skip questions and immediately execute using the optimal settings from the 175-track corpus and 13-Exp Flow lab, attaching a brief summary of the profile with Stage 1.
3. **Stage-1 Quality Gate (`بوابة الجودة`):**
   - In BOTH modes, **never proceed to `EXTEND` (`Stages 2..6`) until `Stage 1 Root` is acoustically and linguistically verified and approved**.

## Permanent Negative Constraints (`NC1–NC25`) & 4-Axis Taxonomy (`V56.0`)

1. **`NC1` (Axis-1 Pure-Vocal vs. Axis-3 Studio Separation):** Never name instruments (`Ney, Flute, Oud, Guitar, Violin, Cello, Piano, Drum Kit`) in `Axis 1` pure-vocal prompts. In `Axis 3` modern studio tracks (`modern_studio_ney_cello_strings_60bpm_saba_bayati_v56` / `golden_studio_*_60bpm_saba_bayati_v56`), use `60 BPM` Maqam Saba/Bayati intro conditioning while strictly banning dance/tarab beats, guitars, pianos, and drum kits.
2. **`NC2, NC16 & NC17` (`23.5s` Extend Window & 3-Vertical-Line Kasra Law):** Each `Extend` window is `23.5s` (`~7.5s` per line at `60 BPM`). Every `Nazla` (`XA`) and `Kasra` (`XB`) in `EXTEND` MUST be **strictly 3 vertical lines (`~21.5s`)** (no `(...)` repeat line in `XB`) so `بيت القفلة` is never cut off (`NC16`). Always purge `Compose > Lyrics` (`Ctrl+A -> Delete`) before every `Extend` (`NC17`).
3. **`NC3–NC15` (Verified Iraqi G2P, Register & Dialect Locks):** Write `انْگِسَمْ نُصَّينْ`; `رِدَتْ نَشْدَه`; `صُوتْ أُمِّ البَنِينْ`; `إِجَتَه .. أُمّ الگَمَرْ وَتْصِيحْ`; `رَدْ لِدْيَارَهْ لَوْ بَعْدَهْ`; `لَمَّنْ رَدْ إِبِنْ حَذْلَمْ`; `بِشِرْ گَطَّعِتْ إِفَّادِي`; `مَا أَنْشِدَكْ إِعْلَه أَولَادِي` (never `عَلْأَوْلَادِي`). Ban high-octave screaming (`NC9`) and enforce terminal Sukun (`NC10`).
4. **`NC18–NC25` (V55.0 & V56.0 13-Experiment Flow Music Laws):**
   - **`NC18–NC22`:** Ban raw DSP Hz numbers (`53Hz`, `55Hz`) and percussion nouns (`chest slaps`, `drums`); lock `Model = Lyria 3.5`; keep `Remix > Cover` `Strength <= 0.40–0.45`; never place `..` inside a 3-word clause before a stranded word (`الخِدْمَه إِنْكِسَارِي وَ دَمْعِتِي`); before `الـ`, write open `-a` endings with `ى / ا` (`يِنْسَى الوَفَا`, `عَلَى التَّرَايِبْ`) and liaison Kasra (`مُو مِثِلِ البَشَرْ`).
   - **`NC23` (Direct Mustahall Entry & Anti-Gasping Law):** Never start `[المُسْتَهَلّ]` with a standalone `آااه ..` line paired with `"weeping vocal grief breaks"` (`78s` gasping in `Exp-2`); start directly with the parenthesized refrain `(صُورَة جَسَدْ عَالْغَبْرَه...)` and `"warm deep emotional Arabic male baritone lead vocal, controlled dignified middle-register mournful delivery"`.
   - **`NC24` (Colloquial Un-Damma'd `أُمّ` & Kasra `آهْ` Removal):** Write `أُمّ البَنِينْ` and `أُمّ الأَرْبَعَه` without Fusha Damma (`أُمُّ` stretched in `Exp-5`), and omit extra `آهْ` before `هَذَا الحُسَينْ / هَذَا الشَّمِرْ` in Kasra (cures trailing `-i` release `التَّرَايِبِي / لَوَاهِبِي`).
   - **`NC25` (`"chest-beat"` & Cover Moderation Firewall):** Use `"bare chest thumping"` instead of `"chest-beat"` (avoids studio rimshots); generate explicit martyrdom poems via `Compose > Lyrics` rather than `Remix > Cover` (`Edit Lyrics` blocks `مَذْبُوحْ`).

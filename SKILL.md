## Mandatory Pre-Generation Protocol: Interactive Consultation vs. Auto-Pilot (`Modules 66, 70–72`)

1. **Default Mode — Interactive Consultation (`الوضع الافتراضي: التشاور التفاعلي قبل التوليد`):**
   - **Strictly FORBIDDEN** to start generating audio or prompts before a fast clarification session with the user, **UNLESS** the user explicitly commands Full Auto-Pilot (`"اختر كل شيء بنفسك"` / `"نفّذ بالوضع التلقائي الكامل"`).
   - **Step 1 (5-Stage Analysis):** Run `recommend_tor_and_rhythm_for_poem()` (`five_stage_analysis`) for meter, dramatic axis, Maqam contour, BPM, and dialect (`عراقي، بحراني، فصيح`).
   - **Step 2 (Actionable Questions Across 4 Axes):** Confirm: (1) **School & 4-Axis Style** (`Axis 1` Pure Human Voice/Choir/Latm, `Axis 2` Traditional Mawkib Dammam/Tasa/Brass, `Axis 3` Modern Studio Ney/Cello/Violins non-dance, `Axis 4` Sub-Bass/Pads & 6-Type Karbala Foley); (2) **Melody & Tor** (`الصبا، البيات، الحجاز، اللامي، الكرد، النهاوند`); (3) **Rhythm & BPM** (`56–60` Thaqeel, `72–84` Mawkib, `115–133` Shoor, `0` Rubato Na'i); (4) **SFX & Reverb**; (5) **Solo vs. Unison Choir `(...)`**.
2. **Auto-Pilot Exception:** If explicitly delegated (`"اختر كل شيء بنفسك"`), execute immediately with the optimal corpus/lab profile and summarize it with Stage 1.
3. **Stage-1 Gate & Mandatory `20s` Chunk Audit (`NC29`):** Never proceed to `EXTEND` or approve ANY generated audio before slicing it via `ffmpeg -f segment -segment_time 20` and verifying dialect authenticity, word integrity, and Arabic Makharij (`ح، ع، ص، ظ، ض، گ، تش/چ`) chunk-by-chunk.

## Permanent Negative Constraints (`NC1–NC29`) & `V57.0` Phonetic Locks

1. **`NC1` (Axis-1 Pure-Vocal vs. Axis-3 Studio):** Never name instruments in `Axis 1` pure-vocal prompts. In `Axis 3` modern studio tracks (`modern_studio_ney_cello_violin_lament_60bpm_saba_lami_v56`), strictly ban dance/tarab beats, pianos, guitars, and Western drum kits.
2. **`NC2, NC16 & NC17` (`23.5s` Extend Window & 3-Line Kasra Law):** Every `Nazla` (`XA`) and `Kasra` (`XB`) in `EXTEND` MUST be **strictly 3 vertical lines (`~21.5s`)** (no `(...)` repeat in `XB`); always purge `Compose > Lyrics` (`Ctrl+A -> Delete`) before every `Extend`.
3. **`NC3–NC15` (Verified Archival Iraqi G2P & Register Locks):** Ban high-octave screaming (`NC9`), enforce dialect isolation (`NC10`), and preserve verified archival spellings in `--compile-lyrics`.
4. **`NC18–NC25` (`V55–V56` Flow Music Laws):** Ban raw Hz numbers (`53Hz`, `55Hz`) & `"chest-beat"` (`NC18/NC25`, use `"bare chest thumping"`); lock `Model = Lyria 3.5` (`NC19`); cap `Remix > Cover` `Strength <= 0.45` (`NC20`); ban mid-clause `..` before stranded words (`NC21`); use `ى / ا` before `الـ` (`NC22`); start `[المستهل]` directly with the refrain (`NC23`, zero opening `آااه ..` gasping line); remove Fusha Damma on `أُمّ` & extra `آهْ` in Kasra (`NC24`); generate martyrdom poems in `Compose > Lyrics` (`NC25`).
5. **`NC26` (Clean Natural Arabic Orthography — Anti-Token-Shattering Law for `Lyria 3.5`):** NEVER feed 100%-over-diacritized text, terminal Sukun spam (`ْ`), or synthetic `إِمْنِ / إِعْلَه / إِتْرَجِمْهَا / عَلَه` into `Lyria 3.5` (causes token shattering: `إِمْنِ->إِبْنِي`, `فَلْسَفَة->فَلْسَفَتِي/فَلْسَفْأَتِ`, `الصُّوَرْ->الصُّؤُورْ`). ALWAYS run **`--compile-clean-lyria`** (`format_clean_lyrics_for_lyria35`) keeping natural spelling + 1–2 surgical vowels ONLY (`الصُوَر`, `فَلْسَفة`, `حگّه`, `گَلبه`, `لَواهب`, `الوَكْت`, `تْرَجِّمها .. أُمّ البنين`).
6. **`NC27` (`چ -> تش` Phonetic Digraph & Clean `گ` Law):** Because `Lyria 3.5` collapses Persian Unicode `چ` to `ك/ج`, convert `چ -> تش` (`تبچي->تبتشي`, `حچي->حَتشِي`, `ملچوم->مَلتشوم`) and use clean un-cluttered `گ` (`وگَلبه`, `گبلك`, `حگّه`, `أوگف`) for authentic `/tʃ/` and `/g/`.
7. **`NC28` (Anti-Pop Regional Dialect Vocal Lock):** Ban generic `"warm deep emotional Arabic male baritone"` in pure Latmiyas (causes Syrian/Levantine pop + piano); use **`v57_iraqi_karbalaei_pure_majlis_60bpm_saba_lami`** or **`v57_bahrani_matam_pure_58bpm_saba_bayati`** (`immediate rhythmic chorus entry, sharp crisp Arabic pharyngeal pronunciation, zero musical instruments, no piano, no pop singing`).
8. **`NC29` (Mandatory `20s` Blind Chunk Phonetic & Makharij Audit):** Slice every generated clip into `20s` segments via FFmpeg and audit word-by-word before approving (reject any track with garbled verses, Levantine drift, or broken Makharij).

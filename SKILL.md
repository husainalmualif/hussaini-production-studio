## Mandatory Pre-Generation Protocol: Consultation vs. Auto-Pilot (`Modules 66, 70–73`)

1. **Default Mode — Interactive Consultation (`الوضع الافتراضي: التشاور التفاعلي`):**
   - **Strictly FORBIDDEN** to generate audio/prompts before clarifying choices with the user, **UNLESS** the user explicitly commands Full Auto-Pilot (`"اختر كل شيء بنفسك"`).
   - **Step 1 (5-Stage & Muwashah Analysis):** Run `recommend_tor_and_rhythm_for_poem()` and `analyze_multi_meter_muwashah_structure()` to detect single-meter vs. multi-meter Muwashah shifts, Common Jins Maqam arc, BPM gears, and dialect (`عراقي، بحراني، فصيح`).
   - **Step 2 (Actionable Questions Across 4 Axes):** Confirm: (1) **School & 4-Axis Style** (`Axis 1` Pure Human Voice/Choir/Latm, `Axis 2` Mawkib Dammam/Tasa/Brass, `Axis 3` Modern Studio Ney/Cello/Violins non-dance, `Axis 4` Sub-Bass/Pads & 6-Type Karbala Foley); (2) **Melody, Maqam Arc & Multi-Meter Transitions** (`الصبا، البيات، الحجاز، اللامي، الكرد، النهاوند`); (3) **Rhythm & BPM Gears** (`56–60` Thaqeel, `72–84` Mawkib/Muwashah, `115–133` Shoor, `0` Rubato Na'i); (4) **Generation Method** (`Combined Pre-Conditioning + Multi-Section JSON` in `Producer Chat` [recommended for multi-meter Muwashah] vs. `Compose > Lyrics`).
2. **Stage-1 Gate & Mandatory `20s` Chunk Audit (`NC29`):** Slice every generated clip into `20s` segments (`ffmpeg -f segment -segment_time 20`) and audit dialect, Makharij (`ح، ع، ص، ظ، ض، گ، تْش/چ`), instrument purity (zero piano/pop drums), and tail loops before approving or extending.

## Permanent Negative Constraints (`NC1–NC31`) & `V58.0` Muwashah/JSON Locks

1. **`NC1` (Axis-1 Pure-Vocal vs. Axis-3 Studio):** Ban instruments in `Axis 1`. In `Axis 3` modern studio tracks (`v58_bahrani_muwashah_hybrid_72bpm_bayati_hijaz_saba` / `modern_studio_ney_cello_violin_lament_60bpm_saba_lami_v56`), strictly ban piano, pop drums, rock snare backbeats, dance/darbuka interludes, and Arabic pop singing.
2. **`NC2, NC16 & NC17` (`23.5s` Extend Window & 3-Line Kasra Law):** Keep `Nazla` (`XA`) and `Kasra` (`XB`) in `Compose > Extend` to 3 vertical lines (`~21.5s`) and always purge `Lyrics` before extending.
3. **`NC3–NC25` (Archival G2P & Flow Music Firewall):** Enforce dialect isolation (`NC10`), ban raw Hz numbers & `"chest-beat"` (`NC18/NC25`), lock `Model = Lyria 3.5` (`NC19`), cap `Cover <= 0.45` (`NC20`), ban mid-clause `..` (`NC21`), open `ى/ا` before `الـ` (`NC22`), direct `[المستهل]` entry (`NC23`), and colloquial un-Damma'd `أُمّ` (`NC24`).
4. **`NC26–NC27-B` (Clean Natural Arabic & Surgical `تْش` / `گ` Law):** Compile `Lyria 3.5` lyrics via **`--compile-clean-lyria`** (never 100%-tashkeel or terminal Sukun spam); convert `چ -> تش` (`تبچي->تبتشي`, `باچر->باتشر`) with surgical `تْش` Sukun on medial clusters (`الحچي->الحَتْشِي`, `البچي->البَتْشِي`) so `Lyria 3.5` never splits them into 3 syllables, plus clean un-cluttered `گ` (`گابل`, `گلبي`, `أوگف`).
5. **`NC28–NC29` (Anti-Pop Vocal Lock & Mandatory `20s` Chunk Audit):** Ban generic soft pop adjectives; audit every track in `20s` blind chunks before approval.
6. **`NC30` (Combined Pre-Conditioning + Multi-Section Muwashah JSON in `Producer Chat`):** Never send standalone Pre-Conditioning JSON without Stage-1 lyrics. Use **`--build-muwashah-json`** (`build_v58_combined_muwashah_json`) to send a SINGLE combined JSON payload locking anti-piano/anti-pop rules, English-only `arrangement_sections[]` (Maqam, BPM gears, Qa'da drops), and `NC26/NC27`-clean `lyrics_register_verbatim` — proven `9.4/10` (`M2-B`) vs. `4.2/10` (`Compose > Lyrics`) on Nader Al-Tattan's multi-meter «بكاء الورق».
7. **`NC31` (Stage-2 Dynamic JSON Extend & Tail-Loop Trim Law):** Extend ONLY from a verified piano-free Stage-1 candidate using `lyrics_panel_action: "OVERWRITE_WITH_STAGE_2_ONLY"`, then audit `20s` chunks and trim any trailing restart loop cleanly before EBU R128 mastering.

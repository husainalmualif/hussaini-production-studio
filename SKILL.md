## Mandatory Pre-Generation Protocol: Consultation vs. Auto-Pilot (`Modules 66, 70–74`)

1. **Default Mode — Interactive Consultation (`الوضع الافتراضي: التشاور التفاعلي`):**
   - **Strictly FORBIDDEN** to generate audio/prompts before clarifying choices with the user, **UNLESS** the user explicitly commands Full Auto-Pilot (`"اختر كل شيء بنفسك"`).
   - **Step 1 (5-Stage, Muwashah & Fingerprint Analysis):** Run `recommend_tor_and_rhythm_for_poem()`, `analyze_multi_meter_muwashah_structure()`, and `select_melodic_fingerprint_v60()` to break melodic monotony and detect multi-meter shifts, 13-Maqam Common Jins arc, BPM gears, and dialect (`عراقي، بحراني، فصيح`).
   - **Step 2 (Actionable Questions Across 4 Axes & 12 Fingerprints):** Confirm: (1) **School & 4-Axis Style** (`Axis 1` Pure Acapella/Latm, `Axis 2` Mawkib Dammam/Tasa, `Axis 3` Zero-Piano Studio Dark Bowed Bass Cello Drone/Reed Ney, `Axis 4` Sub-Bass & 6-Type Karbala Foley, or `Devotional Tawashih`); (2) **Maqam Arc** (`الصبا، الصبا زمزم، البيات، البيات شوري، الحجاز، الحجاز كار، اللامي، الكرد، النهاوند، النوى أثر، السيكاه، الرست، العجم`); (3) **Rhythm & BPM Gears** (`56–60` Thaqeel, `64–76` Cradle/Muwashah/Thulathi, `86–96` Tawashih/Mawkib/Haidari, `124–132` Shoor, `0` Rubato Na'i); (4) **Method** (`Combined Pre-Conditioning + Muwashah JSON` in `Producer Chat` vs. `Compose > Lyrics`).
2. **Stage-1 Gate, Spectral Forensics & Mandatory `20s` Chunk Audit (`NC29/NC33/NC34`):** Run `--detect-artifacts` and `--detect-tail-loop`, slice every generated clip into `20s` segments (`ffmpeg -f segment -segment_time 20`), and audit dialect, Makharij (`ح، ع، ص، ظ، ض، گ، تْش/تش`), zero piano/pop leakage, and splice/tail integrity before approving or extending.

## Permanent Negative Constraints (`NC1–NC34`) & `V60.0` Production Locks

1. **`NC1` (Axis-1 Pure Acapella vs. Axis-3 Zero-Piano Studio):** Ban all instruments in `Axis 1` (`Exp 24-A/B`, `9.7/10`, `0%` instruments). In `Axis 3`, use **`"dark bowed bass cello drone, mournful reed ney, strictly percussive bare chest thuds, subtle acoustic desert wind, strictly zero piano, zero keyboard chords, no pop drums, no snare backbeat"`** (`Exp 22`, `9.8/10`, `0.00%` piano — ban classical `"solo cello"`).
2. **`NC2–NC25` (Extend Budget, Dialect Isolation & Flow Firewall):** Keep `Compose > Extend` Nazla/Kasra to 3 vertical lines (`NC16/17`), enforce single-dialect lock (`NC10`), ban raw Hz & `"chest-beat"` (`NC18/25`), lock `Lyria 3.5` (`NC19`), cap `Cover <= 0.45` (`NC20`), open `ى/ا` before `الـ` (`NC22`), direct `[المستهل]` entry (`NC23`), and un-Damma'd `أُمّ` (`NC24`).
3. **`NC26–NC27-C` (Clean Natural Arabic, Phonological Affricate Sukun Law & `<= 4.2 syl/s` Cap):** Compile via **`--compile-clean-lyria`**; place `تْش` Sukun **ONLY after a short vowel** (`الحَتْشِي`, `البَتْشِي`); **NEVER** place `تْش` Sukun after long vowels (`ا/و/ي`), Sukun consonants (`بْ+تْ`), or word-initially (`باتشر`, `يبتشي`, `تبتشي`, `تشنت`, `تشفوفك` — prevents `باتْشِر->باشر` / `يبتْشي->يبتي`); lock elisions (`بلهيبه->بِلَهيبه`); enforce `<= 4.2 syl/s` on fast meters.
4. **`NC28–NC31` (Anti-Pop Lock, `20s` Blind Chunk Audit & Combined Muwashah JSON):** Audit every track in `20s` blind chunks (`NC29`); send ONE Combined Pre-Conditioning + Muwashah JSON (`--build-muwashah-json`, `NC30`); extend ONLY from verified piano-free takes via `OVERWRITE_WITH_STAGE_2_ONLY` (`NC31`).
5. **`NC32` (Live `Remix > Replace` Surgical Inpainting):** Never regenerate a `9.4+` track for 1–2 mispronounced words; use `--plan-replace` (`8–18s` phrase window, proven `Exp 21-A`, `9.7/10`).
6. **`NC33–NC34` (Spectral Forensics, `90s` Extend Headroom, Auto Tail-Loop Trim & Mid/Side Vocal Stacking):** Run `--detect-artifacts` (`NC33`) and `--detect-tail-loop` (`NC34`); give Stage-2 Extend `90s` headroom (`Exp 24-B`, `4:18`); master locally with `--auto-trim-tail-loop --vocal-stacking [--vintage-intro-sec N]` to `-14.0 LUFS / -1.0 dBTP`.
